Rkd Launcher is a minimalist, text-first home screen. Keep the time, the apps you choose and a clear view of your day without a feed or ads.

<b>Home screen</b>
* Clock, date, battery, a compact screen-time line and optional current weather.
* Show upcoming events from the calendars you select, with a compact mode for more events.
* Choose up to 16 fast apps in one or two columns. Phone and camera stay in the corners.
* Show a selected wallpaper behind the text, with visibility and brightness controls.
* Keep a to-do checklist: add tasks one at a time, hide checked tasks, view and uncheck them later. Checked tasks are removed after seven days.
* Export and restore your launcher settings when changing phones or installations.

<b>Every app, one swipe away</b>
* Search app names, initials and typos. Pin apps to the top of the list.
* Sort A to Z, by most used or by most recent. With a work profile, personal and work apps get their own tabs.
* Rename or hide apps and choose which appear as fast apps on the home screen.

<b>Limits that hold</b>
* Social media apps and games get a daily allowance (30 minutes by default). Set a limit for other apps too. Mail, browsers and messengers are never limited automatically.
* When time is used up, the app locks. You can continue after a pause, and Rkd Launcher asks again before later visits that day.
* See how often you went past a limit in the weekly review.

<b>A weekly look back</b>
* Review screen time, apps, time of day and limit overrides. Write one line for next week.

<b>Privacy</b>
* No account, ads or analytics. Optional weather sends approximate location to Open-Meteo.
* Usage access counts screen time. The optional accessibility service detects the foreground app to enforce limits; it does not read screen contents.
* Calendar access is optional and shows selected events on your home screen.
