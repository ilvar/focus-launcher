Apps

Every app, one swipe away. None of them shouting.

  • Search understands names, initials and typos.
  • New apps sit on top for their first 24 hours.
  • Sort A–Z, by most used or most recent. Work apps get a tab.
  • Long-press an app to time, rename, hide or pin it.

App timers

When time is up, it is up.

  • Social media and games get 30 minutes a day, or what you set.
  • Mail, browsers and messengers are never limited unless you ask.
  • Time's up: closing is the big button, carrying on takes a pause.
  • Ignored a limit? Rkd Launcher still asks before every visit.

What a minimalist launcher is, and what this one adds.

A minimalist launcher swaps the icon grid for a plain list of app names.

Privacy

Local settings, optional weather.

Screen-time and checklist data stay on the phone. If you enable Weather, Rkd Launcher sends approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo for current conditions.

It needs a few switches. Here is why:

Install

Three minutes, start to finish.

  1. Download {{APK_FILE}} ({{SIZE}}).
  2. Open it. Allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Rkd Launcher Settings → Setup. Flip the three switches.
  4. Press Home. Swipe left for apps. Long-press to change things.

A switch is greyed out? App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings.

Questions

Fair things to ask.

Is Rkd Launcher free?

Yes. No price, no subscription, no ads.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone with Android 8.0 or newer.

How is this different from Android's own Digital Wellbeing?

It hides its numbers in settings. Rkd Launcher puts them on your home screen.

Is it safe to install an APK from a website?

Compare the SHA-256 above, and let Play Protect scan the file.

Why does a launcher want an accessibility service?

To lock an app while you are in it. It learns only the app's name.

How does it know what counts as social media?

Its own list of feeds and social networks, plus the Play Store's games.

Will it drain my battery?

No. Idle, it draws zero frames, and nothing runs in the background.

Can I read the source code?

Yes. Free software under GPL-3.0, all of it on GitHub.

Can I have my icons back?

Not here. That is the point. Switching back takes ten seconds.

What about apps in a work profile?

They get their own tab. Android keeps their usage and calendar private.
